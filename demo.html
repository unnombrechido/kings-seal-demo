About King's Seal

King's Seal is not about hiding the message. It is about regulating who can put an official name on a QR code, so emitters are not blamed for deceptive or inconvenient links they never sent.

Three layers, like physical mail: personal trust, official seal, and caution for everything else. The payload stays readable. The HMAC tag only answers one question — did this issuer authorize this exact content?
